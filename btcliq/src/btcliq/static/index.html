      Longs liquidated, price fell (dot under the candle)
      Shorts liquidated, price rose (dot over the candle)
    

    
Levels are estimates: built from changes in open interest on OKX, Hyperliquid, Deribit and Kraken, scaled to
      the whole market (Binance and Bybit totals arrive late via CoinGecko), with an assumed leverage mix. Shorts sit above price,
      longs below, and a level disappears when price reaches it. Dots are real OKX liquidations of $50K or more.
